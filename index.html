Read it

Map — each tile is a sector; bigger tile, bigger market cap; green rising, red falling.

Rotation — sectors drift clockwise: Improving → Leading → Weakening → Lagging. Right = stronger over 7 days, up = stronger today, both vs the median sector. The line is the selected sector's path over recent hours.

Replay — press play to watch the sectors drift over recent days.

Method

Sector 24h change is CoinMarketCap's market-cap change for the category. Sector 7d change is the market-cap-weighted 7-day change of its largest constituents. Snapshots refresh every two hours; only the window needed for display is kept.
